# AI Attendance System - Neo-Minimalist Slate Design System

## Brand & Style
A clean, premium, high-contrast dark-mode SaaS theme modeled after modern developer tools like Vercel and Linear. It prioritizes structure, typography hierarchy, sharp geometries, and clean interactive elements.

## Design Tokens
- **Base Background**: `#08090c` (Deep Slate-Black)
- **Surface Panels**: `#0f111a` (Elevated dark slate cards)
- **Inner Elevated Surface**: `#161824`
- **Primary Accent**: `#3b82f6` (Cobalt Blue for active selections and buttons)
- **Secondary Accent**: `#6366f1` (Indigo Blue for student features)
- **Borders**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (Thin, crisp border separators)
- **Strong Borders**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`
- **Success Badge**: `#10b981` (Emerald Green)
- **Danger Badge**: `#ef4444` (Crimson Red)

## Typography
- **Headlines & UI Titles**: 'Plus Jakarta Sans'
- **Body & Secondary Labels**: 'Inter'
- **Technical Outputs**: 'JetBrains Mono' (used only for monospaced logs)

## Component Specifications
- **Corners**: Tight `8px` corner radius for standard panels and inputs.
- **Webcam Feed**: Clean, minimalist video container with a 1px solid white border, thin cobalt corner brackets, and a status label (`● Live Feed`). No rotating reticles or laser scan lines.
- **Buttons**: High-contrast solid fills (e.g. solid white with black text, or cobalt with white text) and thin-bordered secondary variants. No heavy outer glows.
- **Dividers**: Subdued thin gray lines separating dashboard sections.
- **SVG Charts**: Solid curved lines with clean fill gradients under the curve.
